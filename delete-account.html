Delete your Traveller's Word account

You can delete your Traveller's Word account, and everything stored with it, at any time: in the app, or right here in your browser.

  1. Open Traveller's Word: the app, or the game on this website.
  2. Tap ONLINE DUELS and sign in with your email and password.
  3. Tap PROFILE, then DELETE ACCOUNT, and confirm with DELETE.

If you are in the middle of a duel, finish it or yield it first.

What is deleted

Your sign-in (email and password), your traveller name, rating and duel record, your duel history and the journey saved online. It happens straight away and can't be undone.

A journey saved on your own phone stays there until you uninstall the game or erase it in Settings.

Forgot your password? Use FORGOT PASSWORD? on the sign-in screen to get back in first.
